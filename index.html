Ocean tones

Move across the image to hear temperature. Cooler water plays lower notes; warmer water plays higher notes. Land and missing data are silent.

Synthetic temperature demo — not satellite observations.

Explore the image Sound off

Enable sound, then hover or drag across the image. Keyboard: focus the image and use arrow keys; hold Shift for larger steps. Escape mutes. Start with low volume.

NASA mode uses the GHRSST MUR sea surface temperature image and its published color palette from NASA GIBS. Temperatures are approximate, inferred from image colors, not raw measurements.
